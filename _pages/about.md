## Projects

### As-Rigid-As-Possible Deformation of Gaussian Radiance Fields

An interactive method for as-rigid-as-possible (ARAP) deformation of objects represented by 3D Gaussian Splatting. After geometric editing, the method optimizes the Gaussians to match the deformed radiance field, reducing rendering artifacts while preserving visual quality and efficient rendering. Radial features and an adaptive anisotropic spatial low-pass filter help maintain radiance-field consistency during large deformations.

[Project website](https://xinhaot.github.io/ARAP-Deformation-of-Gaussian-Radiance-Fields/)
